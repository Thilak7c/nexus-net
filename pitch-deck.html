!The challengeProblem statement #1 · by Cradle (Startup ASEAN)

“How can Startup ASEAN successfully convert ecosystem connections into measurable outcomes, including investments, commercial partnerships, market expansion, and cross-border POCs?”

Hacktoberfest Hack Day KL 2026 · Problem statement 1: Outcome Conversion

What Cradle has today
  • Summits, open days and matching sessions create thousands of introductions across 10 countries.
  • A regional platform that lists programmes, funding, talent and partners.
What it can't see
  • Which of those introductions became a meeting, a POC, a partnership or an investment.
  • Which countries, events and programmes actually convert.

1Feature 1Company registration + passport

Every outcome starts with a verified company

Gemma 4 · vision
  • Founders register and upload a registration document (PNG, JPEG or WebP).
  • Gemma 4 compares the company name, registration number and country with the profile.
  • Approved, rejected or unclear, with a reason. Approval issues a company passport; editing details means verifying again.
Solves

Outcomes Cradle can trust. Every quest, XP point and deal is tied to a real, checked company, so the numbers mean something.

3Feature 3AI quest generator

Every connection gets a concrete next step

Gemma 4 · text
  • Gemma 4 reads the startup profile plus the programme and event list.
  • It writes five quests: at least three cross-border, climbing from connect to funding.
  • Each quest names the proof needed. XP comes from a fixed table, never from the model.

“No next step.” An intro in KL becomes “turn your Ho Chi Minh City contact into a POC proposal”: specific, cross-border, doable in 2–4 weeks.

4Feature 4Quest proof verification

Only proven outcomes earn XP

Gemma 4 · vision
  • Founders upload proof: a signed LOI, a meeting invite, an acceptance email.
  • Gemma 4 checks the document type, named parties, country and date (today's date is in the prompt).
  • Approval also needs a named counterpart and a document type, so a random photo can't pass.
Solves

“Measurable.” An outcome only counts once a document backs it, so Cradle's numbers are evidence, not self-reports.

!Today's limits

  • Startups, talent, proof images and dashboard numbers are fictional demo data.
  • Needs questions are fixed; Gemma only words the options.
  • Gemma's confidence isn't calibrated (it reports ~1.0), so approval also needs named parties and a document type.
  • The demo video runs in demo/simulation mode because of API quota.

↑Next steps

  • Plug into real Startup ASEAN programme, event and startup data.
  • A Cradle reviewer queue for low-confidence or high-value proofs.
  • Corporate-side quests, so partners post the pilots they want.
  • Quests and follow-ups in the founder's own language.
